# Jackpot Dive

Portrait pixel-art endless dive. Swipe left and right to catch fish, grab oxygen tanks and dodge jellyfish, eels, sharks and gulpers. Fill the bar for a Jackpot Rush. Every new species joins your aquarium.

Play: https://hkcmadao.github.io/jackpot-dive/

Best on a phone in portrait. On desktop, steer with the left and right arrow keys. Runs here are offline: no leaderboard, and the aquarium is saved in your browser.
